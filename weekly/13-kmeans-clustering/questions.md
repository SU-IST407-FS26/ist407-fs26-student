# Week 13 — Participation Questions (k-Means & Cluster Evaluation)

These are the questions I may call on you to answer in class this week. **Prepare answers in
advance** — they are all answerable from this week's lecture. Participation is graded (see the
syllabus).

## Monday

1. What is a centroid?
2. How does the k-means algorithm work?
3. What is "inertia"?
4. What is the shape of the clusters expected by k-means?
5. Why might k-means converge to a "bad" clustering?
6. How do we deal with the problem of potentially bad clusters?
7. How do we decide how many clusters to use with k-means?
8. How does one interpret a silhouette plot?
9. What is stability analysis?
10. What is the best method to use to determine the correct number of clusters in general?
11. The silhouette score for a single point compares two distances. What are they, and what does it
    mean if a point's score is close to 1, close to 0, or negative?
12. What two things does the Davies-Bouldin index compare for a pair of clusters? Why is a lower
    score better, and what would make it go up?
