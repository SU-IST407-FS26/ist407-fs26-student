# Week 10 — Participation Questions (Decision Trees)

These are the questions I may call on you to answer in class this week. **Prepare answers in
advance** — they are all answerable from this week's lecture. Participation is graded (see the
syllabus).

## Monday

1. Roughly speaking, what is a decision tree?
2. Within a decision tree we need to "split" the data at each level. What is the difference between
   a good split and a bad split?
3. What is the Gini index of a node? What does a Gini of 0 mean? For two classes, what is the
   largest it can be, and what does that mean?
4. How does the decision tree algorithm use the Gini index to choose a split?
5. Name two strategies for handling continuous features in a decision tree.
6. What kind of decision boundary does a decision tree fit? Can it fit a linear decision boundary?
7. Why do decision trees need so little preprocessing (for example, no scaling)?
8. Decision trees are very powerful. What is one pitfall with decision trees?
9. What is one way to overcome that pitfall?
10. What does the `ccp_alpha` parameter do in a decision tree?
11. As you increase `ccp_alpha` from 0, what would you expect to happen to training and test
    performance?
