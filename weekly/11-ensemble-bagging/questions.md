# Week 11 — Participation Questions (Ensemble Methods: Bagging & Random Forests)

These are the questions I may call on you to answer in class this week. **Prepare answers in
advance** — they are all answerable from this week's lecture. Participation is graded (see the
syllabus).

## Monday

1. What is the law of large numbers, and what does it have to do with ensembles?
2. Describe the difference between a soft and a hard voting classifier.
3. What is bagging?
4. The lecture describes three key properties of bagging. What are they?
5. Explain how a random forest works.
6. Other than training each tree on a different sample of the data, what does a random forest do to
   reduce overfitting?
7. What is the difference between a random forest and a bagging classifier built from decision trees?
8. What is out-of-bag evaluation?
9. When you report the performance of a random forest, would you prefer the OOB score or
   cross-validation? Why?
10. How can random forests be used to select features for other ML models?
11. What are the relative pros and cons of a single decision tree versus a random forest?
